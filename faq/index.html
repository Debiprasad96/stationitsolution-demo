Before you send your enquiry.

Practical answers to help your purchasing team get started.

Can I combine several categories in one enquiry?

Yes. Send stationery, furniture, equipment and facility items in one list. Include quantities and specifications for each line so the team can review the complete requirement.

What if I cannot find the exact product?

Send a photograph, model number or description through WhatsApp. The catalogue is a starting point; our team can discuss suitable options and current availability.

How are pricing and delivery confirmed?

Pricing depends on the product, quantity and supply scope. Share your PIN code and required date, then confirm the final price, charges and delivery schedule in the quotation.

Can I discuss a GeM or institutional requirement?

Mention the procurement route and share the item specifications, quantities and documentation requirements. Product eligibility and the process should be confirmed for the particular enquiry.
